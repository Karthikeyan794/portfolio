# Cover art

Drop an image here and point the track's `cover` at it in `src/data.ts`:

    { title: 'The One', artist: 'Anirudh Ravichander', src: '', art: '...', cover: '/music/art/aa23.jpg' }

It fills the centre of the record. With `cover` empty the drawn gradient in
`art` is used instead.

Film posters and album art are copyrighted — use artwork you have the right to
publish, or keep the drawn gradients.
